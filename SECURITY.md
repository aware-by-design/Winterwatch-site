| Version | Supported |
| --- | --- |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Use GitHub’s [private vulnerability reporting form for Winterwatch](https://github.com/aware-by-design/Winterwatch-site/security/advisories/new) to send your findings to the maintainers. If the form is unavailable, keep the details private until reporting is available.

In the private report, include:

- The affected page, door or file, and the version or date tested.
- A short explanation of the potential impact.
- Minimal steps to reproduce in your own browser or a local copy.
- Relevant browser details and redacted screenshots, if useful.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, personal data or information belonging to other people. Use harmless examples and avoid disrupting the public site.

We will review the report and coordinate a fix and disclosure with you. Issues in linked services should be reported to the service responsible.

For accessibility barriers, content corrections and ordinary bugs, use [Winterwatch-site issues](https://github.com/aware-by-design/Winterwatch-site/issues).
