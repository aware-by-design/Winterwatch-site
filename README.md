# Winterwatch website publication

Winterwatch is a 24-door Advent calendar about cybersecurity, AI and privacy. Join NESTOR, your hedgehog guide, to explore real incidents and practise with fictional scenarios. Make three decisions and take away one habit to practise on your own or with your team.

**The first door opens on 1 December 2026 at 09:00 CET.** A new door opens at the same time each day through 24 December.

[Open the Winterwatch calendar](https://aware-by-design.github.io/Winterwatch-site/)

## Feedback and security

- [Public feedback](https://github.com/aware-by-design/Winterwatch-site/issues) is for ordinary bugs, accessibility barriers and content corrections. Include the page or door and a short description. Anyone can read your report as soon as you submit it. Leave out personal information and quiz solutions.
- If you find a security vulnerability, follow the [private reporting instructions in SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer reviews each report and decides whether to approve it for work.

## About the material

This content has been developed using AI tools by members of the ENISA Ad Hoc Working Group (ARCH AHWG) to support awareness-raising and cyber hygiene initiatives. It does not constitute official ENISA guidance or formal regulatory advice.

Learn more about AR-in-a-Box: https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/awareness-and-cyber-hygiene/ar-in-a-box
